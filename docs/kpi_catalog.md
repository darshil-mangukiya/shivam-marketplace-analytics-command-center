## Sales Index

- **Business definition**: Relative sales scale of a marketplace/product/
  segment within the current dataset, 0–100.
- **Formula**: `gross_sales / max(gross_sales across the same grouping) * 100`, clipped to [0, 100].
- **Grain**: varies by output (transaction, product, marketplace, category, brand).
- **Source fields**: product_sales / gross settlement amount (private).
- **Expected range**: 0–100.
- **Interpretation**: 100 = highest-selling entity in the current view; 0 = no sales activity.
- **Owner/persona**: Marketplace Manager, E-Commerce Analyst.
- **Validation logic**: `tests/test_public_output_schema.py`, `tests/test_nonzero_dashboard_metrics.py`.
- **Dashboard usage**: Executive Overview, Marketplace Performance, Product Intelligence, Performance Drivers.
- **Code reference**: `shared/metrics.py:index_from_values`.

## Units Index

- Same construction as Sales Index, applied to unit quantity instead of gross sales.
- **Code reference**: `shared/metrics.py:index_from_values`, applied to `_units_private_calc`.

## Fee % of Gross

- **Formula**: `abs(total_fee) / gross_sales * 100`, clipped to [0, 300]; 0 when gross ≤ 0.
- **Expected range**: 0–300 (>100% is possible and meaningful on low-price/high-fee items).
- **Interpretation**: Higher = more fee burden relative to sales.
- **Owner/persona**: Finance/Commercial Analyst.
- **Validation logic**: `tests/test_financial_masking.py`.
- **Dashboard usage**: Fees & Refunds, Performance Drivers.
- **Code reference**: `shared/metrics.py:pct_of_gross`.

## Refund % of Gross

- Same formula as Fee % of Gross, applied to `abs(refund_amount)`.
- **Code reference**: `shared/metrics.py:pct_of_gross`.

## Promotion % of Gross

- Same formula as Fee % of Gross, applied to `abs(promotion_amount)`.
- **Code reference**: `shared/metrics.py:pct_of_gross`.

## Net-to-Gross %

- **Formula**: `net_amount / gross_sales * 100`, clipped to [-300, 300] (signed).
- **Interpretation**: Share of gross sales retained after fees, refunds, and promotions.
- **Code reference**: `shared/metrics.py:pct_of_gross(..., signed=True)`.

## Margin Index

- **Formula**: `index_from_values(max(estimated_profit, 0))` — same relative-scale construction as Sales Index, applied to non-negative estimated profit.
- **Code reference**: `shared/public_output_builder.py:add_public_metrics`.

## Estimated Profitability Index

- **Business definition**: Blended 0–100 analytical profitability *signal* — **not audited profit**.
- **Formula**: `0.40*margin_index + 0.25*revenue_quality_score + 0.20*(100 - margin_risk_score) + 0.15*net_to_gross_pct(clipped 0-100)`, clipped to [0, 100].
- **Interpretation**: Composite signal combining relative profit scale, revenue quality, inverse margin risk, and net retention.
- **Owner/persona**: Finance/Commercial Analyst.
- **Validation logic**: `tests/test_profitability_metrics.py`.
- **Code reference**: `shared/public_output_builder.py:add_public_metrics`.

## Margin Risk Score

- **Formula**: `fee_pct*0.35 + refund_pct*0.25 + promotion_pct*0.15 + max(0, 70-net_pct)*0.15 + max(0, 30-margin_pct)*0.10`, clipped [0,100].
- **Bands**: ≥70 Critical Review, ≥45 High Risk, ≥25 Medium Risk, else Low Risk.
- **Owner/persona**: Finance/Commercial Analyst.
- **Validation logic**: `tests/test_margin_risk_score.py`.
- **Code reference**: `shared/profitability.py:margin_risk_score`, `margin_risk_band`.

## Revenue Quality Score

- **Formula**: `net_pct - fee_pct*0.30 - refund_pct*0.25 - promotion_pct*0.15`, clipped [0,100].
- **Bands**: ≥80 Strong, ≥65 Healthy, ≥45 Watch, ≥25 At Risk, else Critical.
- **Owner/persona**: E-Commerce Analyst, BI Manager.
- **Validation logic**: `tests/test_kpi_labeling.py`.
- **Code reference**: `shared/profitability.py:revenue_quality_score`, `revenue_quality_band`.

## Action Priority

- **Business definition**: High/Medium/Low urgency tag attached to `recommended_action`, driven by 8 ordered deterministic rules (see `docs/business_rules.md`).
- **Owner/persona**: All personas — the action layer is the shared decision surface.
- **Validation logic**: `tests/test_recommendations.py`, `tests/test_high_priority_action_count.py`.
- **Code reference**: `shared/recommendations.py:ACTION_RULES`, `add_actions`.

## Performance-Driver Variance Metrics

- **Absolute Variance**: `current_value - previous_value` for any headline metric above, computed per marketplace/product for the two most recent **VALID ACTIVITY** `transaction_month` values — a month is valid when its aggregated `order_count` (summed across every marketplace) is `> 0`. A trailing calendar month with zero aggregated order activity (e.g. an in-progress or not-yet-populated month) is excluded from selection rather than compared against; it is never deleted from the source data, and is reported informationally via the `excluded_trailing_periods` column on `mart_performance_driver_summary`, never described as an error.
- **Percentage Variance**: `(current - previous) / abs(previous) * 100`; `None`/blank when `previous == 0` (never silently treated as 0% or infinite).
- **Contribution Share %**: a dimension value's `abs(abs_variance)` divided by the sum of `abs(abs_variance)` across all dimension values for that metric, ×100.
- **Movement**: `Deterioration` / `Improvement` / `Increase` / `Decrease` / `No Change`, derived per-metric (fee/refund/promotion/margin-risk increasing = deterioration; sales/units/net-to-gross/revenue-quality/profitability decreasing = deterioration).
- **Validation logic**: `tests/test_variance_engine.py` (32 tests).
- **Code reference**: `shared/variance_engine.py` (`valid_activity_periods`, `select_default_periods`, `excluded_trailing_periods`).
