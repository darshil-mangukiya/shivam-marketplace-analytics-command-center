# Power BI Page Specification

The PBIX contains 8 pages.

| Page | Purpose | Main content |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Executive Overview | Summary of marketplace activity and actions | order, marketplace, product, index, ratio, and action KPIs |
| 2. Marketplace Performance | Compare marketplaces and months | sales/units indexes, fee/refund/promotion ratios, monthly trend |
| 3. Product & Category Intelligence | Compare products and groups | product detail, brand/category ranking, sales vs. net-to-gross |
| 4. Fees, Refunds & Promotions | Review revenue-quality pressure | product/group ratios and review candidates |
| 5. Estimated Profitability | Review profitability and margin risk | profitability index, margin index, bands, scores |
| 6. Inventory Health | Review restock and slow-mover signals | inventory bands, unit/sales indexes, priority tables |
| 7. Action Center | Prioritize recommendations | action, priority, reason, and counts |
| 8. Validation & Privacy | Review data-quality status | validation checks and output profile |

Filters use transaction month, marketplace, category group, fulfillment type, public product ID, and recommended action where applicable.

All visuals use the six public CSVs listed in [README.md](README.md).
